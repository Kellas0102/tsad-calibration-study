# Evidence-bounded proposed addition (not yet merged)

## Developer-led challenge tests and public-case applicability

We evaluated the executable subset and the documentation workflow separately.
The controlled challenge suite used 60 synthetic structured-evidence bundles, with
expected labels stored separately before executing a hash-frozen version of the
minimal rule engine. The evidence adapter computed artifact identity from actual file
bytes and combined metric postprocessing, threshold-source and temporal-access
metadata. This did not evaluate unrestricted natural-language extraction. The
fixtures were created by the same developer-assisted workflow after inspecting the
rules, so this was neither a blinded nor an independent external validation set.

The engine identified all 16 isolated contradictions of implemented types, retained
all 24 valid controls within its listed checks, and returned not-evaluable for all
eight incomplete-only cases. However, in eight mixed contradiction/missing-evidence
cases, its missing-field short circuit suppressed the known contradiction. Four scope
probes involving equivalence or cross-site generalization received within-scope
outputs because these inferences were not implemented in the minimal engine. We
retain these failures as coverage limits rather than treating executable status
aggregation as complete scientific adjudication. Four paired positive-affine score
controls additionally preserved recalibrated prediction sets.

A purposive ten-report panel drawn from the already-cited literature comprised four
detector reports, three evaluation reports and three benchmark/toolkit reports.
All ten were retrieved after preserving two initial access failures and obtaining
alternative sources for the same reports. A single AI-assisted extraction recorded
60 claim-relative fields with report hashes, PDF-page locators and short quotations.
Metric descriptions were explicit in nine reports and partial in one. Calibration
or threshold-integration descriptions were explicit in seven, partial in one,
unresolved within the declared search in one and not applicable to one supplied-event
claim. Temporal or aggregation descriptions were located in all ten. These counts
measure document-field locatability, not gate sufficiency, study quality or the
prevalence of flawed practices in the field. Code pointers did not establish original
score identity. The selected statements did not estimate operational-intervention
benefits, so operational-utility assessment was not applicable to this panel.

The cases clarify positive as well as limiting uses of the protocol. Explicitly
reported point adjustment bounds the estimand without by itself constituting
mislabeling. Disclosed oracle-best comparisons and threshold-integrated metrics can
be legitimate retrospective analyses without establishing a deployable threshold.
Published method configurations can also differ from source defaults: MEMTO's
Appendix A specifies ten memory items, while the additional pinned-source-model
group in this study uses 128. Consequently neither that group nor its strict F1
results should be represented as a numerical reproduction of the paper's adjusted
F1 experiments.

This extension does not assess inter-rater reliability, expert consensus, unrestricted
automatic fault-detection accuracy, or improvement in review decisions. The purposive
panel overlaps the protocol-development literature and all extractions remain subject
to accountable-author verification. Unresolved fields identify minimum evidence
requests rather than establish that the authors did not perform the corresponding
steps. Full-text copies are retained locally for source checking; redistribution is
not inferred from download access.
