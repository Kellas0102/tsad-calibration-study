final evidence: the extraction locates quoted passages in retrieved full PDFs.

the NeurIPS 2023 MEMTO proceedings page, arXiv TimesNet abstract and official
OmniAnomaly repository README. Search-result counts were not screened as a population.

counts are recorded in sources.json and retrieval_followup.json. Those records, not
search-engine snippets or third-party generated summaries, identify analyzed reports.

was retrieved instead and is explicitly the analyzed version. TimeEval's initial
guessed filename returned a non-PDF; the publisher-indexed p3678-schmidl.pdf succeeded.
No unreported automatic retries or report exclusions were made.
